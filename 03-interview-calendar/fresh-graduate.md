# Fresh Graduate Interview Roadmap

As a recent graduate, interviewers know you might lack extensive industry experience. Instead, they test your mastery of computer science fundamentals, your ability to learn quickly, and how well you can discuss your academic projects.

## Focus Areas

| Subject | What to Focus On | Expected Depth |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Structures & Algorithms** | LeetCode Easy/Medium. Strong focus on runtime/space complexities. | High |
| **CS Fundamentals** | OS (Threads/Processes, Deadlocks), Networking (TCP/IP), DBMS (SQL queries, Normalization). | Medium-High |
| **Project Deep-Dive** | End-to-end understanding of your capstone or major academic projects. | High |
| **System Design** | Generally not expected, but basic OOD (e.g., Design a Deck of Cards) is common. | Low |

## 4-Week Action Plan

### Week 1: DSA Refresher
- **Action Items**: Review core DS (Arrays, Strings, Linked Lists, Trees).
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Can you confidently explain Big-O notation?
  - [ ] Complete 15-20 LeetCode Easy questions.
  - [ ] Implement a Binary Search Tree from scratch.

### Week 2: Algorithms & CS Fundamentals
- **Action Items**: Sorting, Searching, Recursion. Start revising OS/Networking.
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Review differences between TCP and UDP.
  - [ ] Understand Mutexes, Semaphores, and Deadlocks.
  - [ ] Complete 15 LeetCode Mediums.

### Week 3: Project Polish & Mock Interviews
- **Action Items**: Prepare your project elevator pitch. Practice coding on a whiteboard (or Google Doc without syntax highlighting).
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Structure your project explanation: Problem, Tech Stack, Your Contribution, Impact.
  - [ ] Do 2 peer mock interviews.

### Week 4: Behavioral Preparation
- **Action Items**: Prepare answers for common entry-level behavioral questions.
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] "Tell me about a time you worked on a team and faced a conflict."
  - [ ] "Why do you want to join this company as your first job?"
