# Career Switcher Roadmap

Switching into software engineering from a different field (or via a bootcamp) is highly commendable. Your key challenge is proving your technical competence while leveraging your previous domain experience as a unique strength.

## Strategy

> [!TIP]
> Do not hide your past career. Instead, highlight the transferable skills: communication, project management, domain expertise (e.g., finance, healthcare), and resilience.

## Focus Areas

1. **Demonstrable Code**: Since you lack a traditional CS degree, your GitHub portfolio and deployed projects are your resume.
2. **Practical Knowledge**: Deep understanding of modern frameworks (React, Node, Django, etc.) and building APIs.
3. **Algorithms**: Many switchers skip DSA to learn frameworks. You *must* bridge this gap to pass standardized interviews.

## 6-Week Action Plan

### Weeks 1-2: Bridging the DSA Gap
- Focus purely on the most common patterns: Two Pointers, Sliding Window, BFS/DFS, and Hash Maps.
- Do not spend weeks on esoteric topics (like AVL trees) unless targeting FAANG.
- **Goal**: Comfortably solve LeetCode Easy and common Mediums.

### Weeks 3-4: The Portfolio Polish
- Ensure you have 1-2 "hero" projects. A hero project is a fully deployed, non-trivial application (not just a generic to-do list).
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Add a comprehensive `README.md` to your repo.
  - [ ] Ensure your code handles edge cases and has some unit tests.
  - [ ] Practice explaining your architectural decisions.

### Weeks 5-6: Framing Your Narrative
- Prepare your "Tell me about yourself" pitch to seamlessly transition from your old career to software engineering.
- Highlight times you taught yourself a difficult concept (demonstrating you are a self-starter).
- Do behavioral mock interviews focusing on your career transition rationale.
