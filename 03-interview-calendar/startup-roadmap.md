# Startup Interview Roadmap

Startups (Seed to Series C) look for different qualities than massive corporations. They need engineers who are adaptable, product-minded, and capable of working across the stack without hand-holding.

## What Startups Value

- **Velocity**: Can you ship code quickly?
- **Autonomy**: Can you take vague requirements and figure out a solution?
- **Culture Fit**: Are you passionate about the product domain? Will you thrive in a chaotic environment?

## The Interview Process

Startups often skip the standard LeetCode gauntlet in favor of:
1. **Take-Home Assignments**: Building a small feature or API.
2. **Pair Programming**: Working on real-world bug fixes or feature additions alongside a current engineer.
3. **Past Work Deep Dive**: Walking through a complex system you've built.

## Preparation Plan

### 1. Master Your Stack
- Be extremely comfortable in your primary language and framework. You should be able to spin up a server, connect to a database, and write an endpoint from scratch in minutes.
- **Action**: Build a simple CRUD app entirely from memory.

### 2. Prepare for Take-Homes
- Timebox yourself. Startups evaluate how much you can achieve in a set time.
- Always include: A README, instructions on how to run the code, basic tests, and comments explaining your trade-offs.

### 3. The Product Mindset
- Startups want engineers who care about the user.
- **Action**: Research the startup's product. Come to the interview with thoughts on their UX, potential new features, or technical challenges they might be facing.

### 4. Behavioral Questions
- Focus on stories highlighting your resilience, ability to wear multiple hats, and times you navigated extreme ambiguity.
