# 90-Day Interview Preparation Plan

A 12-week plan is ideal for engineers aiming for top-tier companies (FAANG/Big Tech) who want to leave no stone unturned. This allows for deep foundational study, extensive practice, and multiple mock loops.

## Flowchart: The 12-Week Journey

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Phase 1: Fundamentals (Weeks 1-3)] --> B[Phase 2: Algo Patterns (Weeks 4-7)]
    B --> C[Phase 3: System Design (Weeks 8-10)]
    C --> D[Phase 4: Mocks & Behavioral (Weeks 11-12)]
    
    A1(Data Structures Revamp) --- A
    A2(Big O Notation) --- A
    
    B1(LeetCode Top 150) --- B
    B2(Advanced DP & Graphs) --- B
    
    C1(High-Level Design) --- C
    C2(Deep Dive into Databases) --- C
    
    D1(STAR Framework) --- D
    D2(Full Interview Loops) --- D
```

## Detailed Breakdown

### Phase 1: Fundamentals (Weeks 1-3)
- **Week 1**: Arrays, Strings, Two Pointers, Sliding Window. Implement ArrayList and HashMap from scratch.
- **Week 2**: Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues. Implement LRU Cache.
- **Week 3**: Trees (BST, AVL, Red-Black basics) and Tries.

### Phase 2: Algorithmic Patterns (Weeks 4-7)
- **Week 4**: Graphs (BFS, DFS, Topological Sort, Union-Find).
- **Week 5**: Backtracking, Combinations, Permutations, Subsets.
- **Week 6**: Dynamic Programming (1D, 2D, Knapsack, Longest Common Substring).
- **Week 7**: Heaps, Greedy Algorithms, Bit Manipulation, Interval problems.

### Phase 3: System Design (Weeks 8-10)
- **Week 8 (Core Concepts)**: Proxies, Load Balancers, Consistent Hashing, CAP Theorem, PACELC, Caching Strategies.
- **Week 9 (Data Layer)**: Relational vs Non-Relational, Sharding, Replication, Indexes, B-Trees, Write-Ahead Logging.
- **Week 10 (Case Studies)**: Design WhatsApp, Design Netflix, Design Ticketmaster, Design a Distributed Message Queue.

### Phase 4: OOD, Behavioral, and Mocks (Weeks 11-12)
- **Week 11**: Object-Oriented Design (Design Patterns, SOLID). Crafting leadership stories and refining resume bullet points.
- **Week 12**: Intense Mock Interviews. 1-2 full loops simulating the exhaustion of a 5-hour onsite. Review OS, Networking, and Language-specific trivia.

## Weekly Checkpoints
- Are you able to solve Mediums in <25 minutes?
- Are you driving the system design discussion, or is the interviewer prompting you?
- Are your behavioral stories concise and focused on *your* impact ("I did" vs "We did")?
