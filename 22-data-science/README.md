# Data Analytics Interviews

## Overview
Data Analytics interviews focus on transforming data into actionable business intelligence. Key areas include advanced SQL, data visualization principles, understanding business metrics, and proficiency with BI tools.

## Interview Questions

### Q1: What is the role of a Data Analyst compared to a Data Scientist?
**Difficulty:** Easy | **Frequency:** Medium | **Companies:** All

**Excellent Answer:**
A Data Analyst focuses on understanding the past and present to answer specific business questions. They build dashboards, define KPIs, write complex SQL pipelines, and provide descriptive/diagnostic analytics to stakeholders. 
A Data Scientist tends to focus on predictive and prescriptive analytics, utilizing machine learning algorithms and heavy statistical modeling to forecast the future or automate decisions. Analysts drive business intelligence, while Scientists drive data products and advanced modeling.

**Common Mistakes:**
- Suggesting that Data Analysts don't code or are less technical.
- Blurring the lines too much so the roles seem identical.

## Real-World Applications
- Creating an executive dashboard that tracks company OKRs in real-time.
- Analyzing marketing campaign ROI to reallocate budget.

## Hiring Manager Perspective
I look for candidates with excellent communication skills. A brilliant SQL query is useless if the analyst cannot explain the resulting insights to a non-technical marketing team and influence their decisions.
