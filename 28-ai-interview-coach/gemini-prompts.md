# Gemini Prompts for Interview Coaching

Gemini's integration with real-time information and vast knowledge base makes it excellent for researching companies, understanding recent tech shifts, and quick-fire knowledge checks.

## 1. Company-Specific Pre-Interview Briefing

**Initial Prompt:**
> "I have an engineering interview tomorrow with [Target Company, e.g., Stripe]. 
> 
> Act as an industry analyst and provide a briefing document that covers:
> 1. Their core engineering challenges and tech stack (based on their engineering blog or open-source contributions).
> 2. Recent product launches or major news from the last 6 months.
> 3. What values or leadership principles they emphasize in their culture.
> 4. Three highly tailored, insightful questions I can ask my interviewer at the end of the session to show I've done deep research."

## 2. Rapid-Fire Technical Trivia (Flashcards)

**Initial Prompt:**
> "Act as a demanding technical interviewer. I am preparing for a Senior DevOps Engineer role. We are going to do a rapid-fire knowledge check.
> 
> Ask me 15 rapid-fire questions covering Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, and Linux networking. 
> Ask them one by one. I will provide a short answer. You will tell me if I am right, wrong, or partially correct, provide a one-sentence correction, and immediately ask the next question."

## 3. The "Explain Like I'm 5" (ELI5) Concept Checker

**Initial Prompt:**
> "I need to ensure I deeply understand complex concepts so I can explain them clearly in an interview. 
> 
> I am going to explain the concept of 'Eventual Consistency in Distributed Databases' to you. 
> 
> Act as a Junior Developer who is eager to learn but gets confused by jargon. If I use terms like 'vector clocks' or 'gossip protocol' without explaining them simply, interrupt me and ask for clarification. Grade my explanation on clarity, accuracy, and accessibility."
