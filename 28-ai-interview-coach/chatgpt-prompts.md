# ChatGPT Prompts for Interview Coaching

ChatGPT (especially GPT-4) is excellent for maintaining a conversational state and roleplaying as a strict but fair interviewer.

## 1. Full Behavioral Mock Interview (Roleplay)

**Initial Prompt:**
> "Act as a Senior Engineering Manager at [Target Company, e.g., Meta]. You are interviewing me for a Senior Software Engineer position. We will conduct a 45-minute behavioral interview focused on leadership, conflict resolution, and delivering results.
> 
> Here are the rules:
> 1. Ask me one question at a time.
> 2. Wait for my response before giving feedback or asking the next question.
> 3. If my answer does not clearly follow the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), ask probing follow-up questions to extract that information.
> 4. Do not break character.
> 
> Start the interview by introducing yourself and asking the first question."

**End of Interview Feedback Prompt:**
> "Let's pause the roleplay. Based on my answers so far, provide a comprehensive evaluation using a standard engineering behavioral rubric. Score me out of 5 on: Clarity, Impact, Leadership, and Ownership. Provide one specific strength and one area for improvement for each story I told."

## 2. Technical Coding Mock Loop (Data Structures & Algorithms)

**Initial Prompt:**
> "Act as a Senior Staff Engineer conducting a technical coding interview. I am interviewing for a Mid-Level Backend Engineer role.
> 
> Here is how we will proceed:
> 1. Give me a medium-to-hard LeetCode-style problem focusing on graphs or dynamic programming. Do not tell me the optimal approach.
> 2. Wait for me to ask clarifying questions and propose an approach.
> 3. Once we agree on an approach, I will write the code.
> 4. Review my code for edge cases, time/space complexity, and clean code principles.
> 
> Please give me the first problem."

## 3. Resume Roasting & Experience Extraction

**Initial Prompt:**
> "Here is my current resume: [Paste Resume]
> 
> Act as an aggressive, highly critical tech recruiter. Read my resume and point out:
> 1. Vague bullet points that lack quantifiable impact.
> 2. Technologies I claim to know but don't have evidence of using in my experience section.
> 3. Give me 3 tough questions a hiring manager would ask based on the weakest parts of my resume."
