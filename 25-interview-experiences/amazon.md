# Amazon Interview Experience

## Role/Level
- **Title:** Software Development Engineer II (SDE II)
- **Level:** L5
- **Location:** Seattle, WA

## Application
- **Method:** Recruiter reached out on LinkedIn.
- **Timeline:** Messaged Oct 5, OA sent Oct 8.

## Online Assessment
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Format:** 2 Coding questions on HackerRank + Work Style Assessment.
- **Outcome:** Solved 1 optimally, 1 sub-optimally. Passed.

## Phone Screen
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Topics:** 20 mins behavioral (Tell me about a time you delivered under pressure), 40 mins coding (Sliding window problem).

## Onsite
| Round | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Coding/Behavioral | Object-Oriented Design (Design a Parking Lot) + 2 LPs. |
| 2 | System Design/Behavioral | Design an e-commerce checkout service + 2 LPs. |
| 3 | Coding/Behavioral | Arrays/Hashmaps (Two Sum variant) + 2 LPs (Bar Raiser). |
| 4 | Behavioral | Deep dive into past architecture projects + 2 LPs. |

## Offer Details
- **Base:** $155,000
- **Equity:** 50 RSU (~$150k at the time) back-heavy vesting.
- **Sign-on:** $60k Year 1, $45k Year 2.

## Lessons Learned
- Leadership Principles are no joke. Over half the onsite time was spent on behavioral questions.
- Having a "story bank" mapped to LPs saved me.

## Resources Used
- Dan Croitor's YouTube Channel for LPs.
- Grokking the System Design Interview.
