# Netflix Interview Experience

## Role/Level
- **Title:** Senior Software Engineer
- **Level:** Senior
- **Location:** Los Gatos, CA

## Application
- **Method:** Referred by a connection.
- **Timeline:** Fast-tracked. Onsite scheduled within 2 weeks.

## Online Assessment

## Phone Screen
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Topics:** Deep technical dive into Java concurrency, garbage collection, and Spring Boot internals. No LeetCode.

## Onsite
| Round | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Architecture | System design for a high-throughput video metadata ingestion pipeline. |
| 2 | Technical Deep Dive | Pair programming/debugging a realistic distributed systems issue. |
| 3 | Culture/Behavioral | Intensive discussion on "Freedom & Responsibility". |
| 4 | Culture/Behavioral | Meeting with Director. Discussing risk-taking and candid feedback. |

## Offer Details
- **Base:** $550,000 (Netflix does all-cash compensation, no standard RSUs)

## Lessons Learned
- Unbelievably high bar for domain expertise. You cannot bluff your way through the technical rounds.
- The culture rounds felt like a psychology evaluation to ensure alignment with their values.
