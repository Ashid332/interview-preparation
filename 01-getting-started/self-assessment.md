# Skills Gap Analysis: Self-Assessment

Use this self-assessment to identify your current proficiency across key software engineering interview domains. Be honest. This will serve as the foundation for your study plan.

## Rating Scale
* **1 - Novice**: Unfamiliar or rarely used. Need to start from basics.
* **2 - Beginner**: Understand the theory but struggle to implement it without guidance.
* **3 - Intermediate**: Can implement standard problems but may struggle with edge cases or optimization.
* **4 - Advanced**: Confident in implementing and optimizing under time pressure.
* **5 - Expert**: Deep understanding, can easily teach others and identify complex variations.
