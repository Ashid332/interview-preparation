# Engineering Interview Handbook: Getting Started

Welcome to the Engineering Interview Handbook. This guide is designed to provide a structured, practical approach to preparing for software engineering interviews, ranging from algorithmic problem-solving to system design and behavioral assessments.

## Philosophy of Interview Preparation

Successful interview preparation is not about memorizing solutions to hundreds of problems. Instead, it is built on:
- **Pattern Recognition**: Understanding underlying concepts (e.g., sliding window, two pointers, BFS/DFS) rather than rote memorization.
- **Communication First**: An optimal solution is useless if you cannot explain it. The interview is a collaborative process.
- **Consistency Over Intensity**: Steady, focused practice over weeks yields better results than cramming days before the interview.
- **Realistic Mock Environments**: Practicing under time constraints with a whiteboard or plain text editor is crucial for simulating interview stress.

## How to Use This Handbook

1. **Assess Your Baseline**: Begin with the [Self-Assessment](self-assessment.md) to identify your strong areas and gaps.
2. **Target Weaknesses**: Allocate your study time based on the self-assessment results. Spend 70% of your time on weak areas and 30% maintaining your strengths.
3. **Progressive Overload**: Start with easier problems to grasp patterns, then progressively tackle medium and hard problems.
4. **Mock Interviews**: Incorporate peer mock interviews or platform-based practice early in your preparation cycle.
