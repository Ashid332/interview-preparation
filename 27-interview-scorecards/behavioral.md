# Behavioral Interview Scorecard

This rubric evaluates how candidates have handled past professional situations, their ability to navigate ambiguity, resolve conflicts, and demonstrate emotional intelligence. We expect candidates to use the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method.

## Scoring Rubric

* **5 - Strong Hire:** 
  * **Traits:** Exceptional self-awareness. Discusses failures openly and demonstrates deep learnings. Actions taken were proactive and highly impactful.
  * **Evidence:** Gives incredibly structured STAR answers. Shows high empathy in conflict resolution and took ownership of not just their task, but the team's success.
* **4 - Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Solid, clear examples of past work. Good understanding of their role in previous successes/failures.
  * **Evidence:** Uses STAR method effectively. Handled conflicts professionally. Took reasonable actions to resolve ambiguity.
* **3 - Neutral:**
  * **Traits:** Answers are somewhat vague or generic. Focuses too much on "We" instead of "I" (making it hard to gauge their specific contribution).
  * **Evidence:** Struggles to clearly define the Result of their actions. Avoids discussing failures or blames others slightly for conflicts.
* **2 - Lean No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Fails to provide specific examples. Highly reactive rather than proactive. 
  * **Evidence:** Cannot structure an answer. Shows poor judgment in past conflicts. Needs constant prompting to get to the point.
* **1 - Strong No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Major red flags. Displays arrogance, toxicity, or extreme defensiveness.
  * **Evidence:** Blames former teammates or managers entirely for failures. Refuses to admit mistakes. Unprofessional conduct during the interview.

## Hiring Manager Notes

Behavioral signals are often leading indicators of how a person will act under pressure. For the Hiring Committee:
- Do not ignore a 1 or 2 in this category. Technical brilliance does not offset toxic behavior. 
- A "Strong Hire" here often acts as a multiplier for the rest of the team.
- Ensure the interviewer documented specific quotes or examples, especially for scores 3 and below, to prevent unconscious bias from swaying the decision.
