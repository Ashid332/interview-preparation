# Culture Fit & Values Alignment Scorecard

This rubric evaluates how well the candidate aligns with the company's core values, their adaptability, growth mindset, and overall contribution to a positive work environment. We look for "culture add" rather than just "culture fit."

## Scoring Rubric

* **5 - Strong Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Embodies the core values and brings a diverse, positive perspective that will elevate the team.
  * **Evidence:** Demonstrates an immense growth mindset. Highly adaptable to change. Shows deep passion for the company's mission. Actively champions diversity and inclusion in their past roles.
* **4 - Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Aligns well with company values. Collaborative and open-minded.
  * **Evidence:** Shows willingness to learn. Works well in team settings. Respectful of different opinions. Understands and appreciates the company's goals.
* **3 - Neutral:**
  * **Traits:** Inoffensive but doesn't particularly stand out in terms of values alignment.
  * **Evidence:** Seems indifferent to the company mission (just looking for a job). Prefers rigid structures and might struggle slightly with rapid change. Minimal evidence of cross-functional empathy.
* **2 - Lean No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Misaligned with core values. Shows signs of rigidity or negativity.
  * **Evidence:** Complains extensively about past employers. Highly resistant to feedback. Focuses entirely on individual success over team success.
* **1 - Strong No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Exhibits behaviors directly contradictory to company values.
  * **Evidence:** Discriminatory remarks, extreme arrogance, unethical behavior, or a complete lack of integrity. A clear threat to team psychological safety.

## Hiring Manager Notes

For the Hiring Committee:
- "Culture Fit" should not be an excuse for bias (e.g., "I'd want to get a beer with them"). Focus on **Values Alignment** and **Culture Add**.
- Document specific behaviors and quotes. If you score someone a 2, state exactly what they said that indicated rigidity or negativity.
- A 1 in this category is an immediate veto for the Hiring Committee, regardless of technical prowess. Protecting the team culture is paramount.
