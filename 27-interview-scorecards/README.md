# Engineering Interview Scorecards

Welcome to the Interview Scorecards section of the Engineering Interview Handbook. This directory contains detailed rubrics designed to evaluate engineering candidates consistently and fairly across various dimensions.

## The 1-5 Scoring Scale

Across all rubrics, we use a standardized 1-5 scale to grade a candidate's performance. These scores map to specific hiring recommendations:

* **5 - Strong Hire:** The candidate demonstrates exceptional skills, far exceeding the baseline requirements. They bring additive value or unique strengths to the team and require zero hand-holding.
* **4 - Hire:** The candidate meets all expectations solidly. They are proficient, communicate well, and would be a productive member of the team.
* **3 - Neutral (Mixed):** The candidate has some strengths but also notable weaknesses. They might need significant ramp-up time or mentoring. Usually treated as a "No Hire" unless other interviews strongly compensate.
* **2 - Lean No Hire:** The candidate struggles significantly with the core requirements. They require too many hints or display noticeable gaps in experience/skills.
* **1 - Strong No Hire:** The candidate displays major red flags, such as inability to code, toxic behavioral traits, extreme lack of communication, or fundamental misunderstanding of core concepts.

## Scorecard Categories

1. **[Behavioral](behavioral.md):** Evaluates past experiences, conflict resolution, and handling ambiguity using the STAR method.
2. **[Technical Coding](technical-coding.md):** Assesses problem-solving, data structures, algorithms, code quality, and testing.
3. **[System Design](system-design.md):** Evaluates architecture skills, scalability, trade-off analysis, and system constraints.
4. **[Leadership](leadership.md):** Focuses on mentorship, taking ownership, strategic thinking, and team influence (primarily for Senior+).
5. **[Communication](communication.md):** Measures clarity, active listening, conciseness, and articulation of complex ideas.
6. **[Culture Fit / Values Alignment](culture-fit.md):** Assesses alignment with company values, growth mindset, and collaboration.

## Hiring Manager Notes: The Hiring Committee Decision

When compiling these scores for the Hiring Committee (HC):
- **Consensus is key, but not mandatory:** A single "Lean No Hire" doesn't automatically disqualify a candidate if they have multiple "Strong Hires" in critical areas, but a "Strong No Hire" usually ends the process.
- **Signal over Noise:** The HC looks for strong signals. A candidate with a 5 in Coding and a 4 in System Design but a 3 in Leadership might be a great mid-level engineer, but perhaps not a Staff engineer.
- **Calibrate:** Always include detailed notes justifying the score. A "4" without context is useless to the HC. Quote the candidate where possible.
