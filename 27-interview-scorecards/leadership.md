# Leadership Interview Scorecard

This rubric is typically used for Senior, Staff, and Management roles. It evaluates mentorship, cross-functional collaboration, strategic impact, and the ability to drive consensus among differing opinions.

## Scoring Rubric

* **5 - Strong Hire:**
  * **Traits:** A true force multiplier. Empowers others and shapes team direction.
  * **Evidence:** Shares deep examples of mentoring juniors into seniors. Describes leading complex, cross-team initiatives from inception to delivery. Navigates extreme organizational ambiguity. Fosters a culture of psychological safety.
* **4 - Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Solid technical leader. Takes ownership and delivers reliably.
  * **Evidence:** Good examples of code reviews, documentation, and leading medium-sized projects. Effectively resolves technical disagreements. Steps up when there is a void in leadership.
* **3 - Neutral:**
  * **Traits:** Capable individual contributor, but limited leadership impact.
  * **Evidence:** Focuses mostly on their own tasks. Mentorship is limited to answering questions rather than proactive growth of others. Struggles to influence decisions outside their immediate peers.
* **2 - Lean No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Avoids responsibility. Requires heavy direction.
  * **Evidence:** Blames management for lack of direction. Does not assist teammates. "Not my job" mentality. Cannot articulate how their work aligns with business goals.
* **1 - Strong No Hire:**
  * **Traits:** Destructive to team morale. Hoards knowledge.
  * **Evidence:** Micromanages (if a manager) or refuses to collaborate (if an IC). Demonstrates a "brilliant jerk" persona. Disparages junior engineers.

## Hiring Manager Notes

For the Hiring Committee:
- At the Staff/Principal level, a "4" or "5" in Leadership is absolutely required.
- Beware of candidates who confuse "Leadership" with "Management". True leadership is about influence, not just authority.
- If a candidate scores a 5 in Coding/Design but a 1 or 2 in Leadership for a senior role, HC should strongly consider down-leveling them or rejecting them entirely.
