# Career Toolkit

Welcome to the Career Toolkit section of the Engineering Interview Handbook. As a senior engineering manager, I've seen thousands of resumes, interviewed hundreds of candidates, and extended dozens of offers. This toolkit is designed to give you the insider perspective on how to position yourself for success.

Getting a great engineering job isn't just about passing the technical screens. It's about how you present yourself, how you network, and how you negotiate. Your technical skills get you the job, but your career toolkit gets you the interview and maximizes your offer.

## Contents

1. [Resume Engineering](resume.md) - Crafting a resume that passes the 6-second scan and highlights your true impact.
2. [LinkedIn Optimization](linkedin.md) - Making recruiters come to you.
3. [GitHub Profile](github-profile.md) - Presenting your code in the best light.
4. [Portfolio Development](portfolio.md) - Showing, not just telling, what you can build.
5. [Networking](networking.md) - Building relationships that lead to opportunities.
6. [Cold Outreach](cold-outreach.md) - Reaching out to hiring managers and peers effectively.
7. [Referrals](referrals.md) - Navigating the fastest path to an interview.
8. [Personal Branding](personal-branding.md) - Becoming a known quantity in your niche.
9. [Open Source Contributions](open-source.md) - Accelerating your career through public code.
10. [Salary Negotiation](salary-negotiation.md) - Getting paid what you're actually worth.
11. [Offer Evaluation](offer-evaluation.md) - Looking beyond the base salary to evaluate the true value of an opportunity.

Treat your career like a software system. It requires architecture, maintenance, and continuous improvement. Let's get to work.
