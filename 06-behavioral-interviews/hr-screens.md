# HR Screens

The HR screen is typically the first step in the interview process. It's usually a 30-45 minute call with a recruiter or HR representative to evaluate basic fit, motivation, and communication skills before advancing you to technical rounds.

## Purpose of the HR Screen
* **Verify Qualifications**: Ensure your background matches the resume and job requirements.
* **Assess Culture Fit**: Evaluate if your values align with the company's culture.
* **Gauge Communication Skills**: Check your ability to articulate your experience clearly.
* **Clarify Logistics**: Discuss salary expectations, visa status, notice period, and relocation needs.

## Common HR Screen Questions

### Motivation and Interest
* "Why are you interested in joining our company?"
* "What are you looking for in your next role?"
* "Why are you leaving your current position?"

### Background and Experience
* "Walk me through your resume."
* "Can you tell me about your current role and responsibilities?"
* "What is your most significant professional achievement?"

### Logistics
* "What are your salary expectations?"
* "Are you authorized to work in [Country]?"
* "When would you be able to start?"

## How to Prepare
1. **Research the Company**: Know their mission, recent news, and product offerings.
2. **Review the Job Description**: Be ready to connect your experience to their requirements.
3. **Prepare Your Pitch**: Have a concise and engaging "Tell me about yourself" ready.
4. **Know Your Numbers**: Be clear on your compensation expectations and current package.
5. **Prepare Questions**: Have 1-2 thoughtful questions to ask the recruiter at the end.
