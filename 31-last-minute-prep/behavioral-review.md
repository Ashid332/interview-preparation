# Behavioral Review One-Pager

## The STAR Method Framework
*   **Situation (10%):** Set the context. Provide necessary background but keep it brief.
*   **Task (10%):** What was the specific problem you needed to solve or goal to achieve?
*   **Action (60%):** What did *YOU* do? (Use "I", not "We"). Detail your technical choices, communication, and leadership.
*   **Result (20%):** What was the outcome? Use hard numbers ($$, %, time saved). What did you learn?

## Core Stories Matrix (Mental Hooks)
Have one story ready for each of these themes:
1.  **Challenging Tech Problem:** (Focus on deep technical dive, trade-offs, debugging)
2.  **Conflict with Colleague/Product:** (Focus on empathy, data-driven decisions, compromise)
3.  **Failure/Mistake:** (Focus on taking ownership, post-mortem, preventing recurrence)
4.  **Leadership/Initiative:** (Focus on identifying a gap, rallying a team, delivering beyond scope)
5.  **Tight Deadline:** (Focus on prioritization, scoping down, communicating risk)

## "Tell Me About Yourself" (The Elevator Pitch)
Keep it to 90-120 seconds.
1.  **Present:** "Currently, I am..." (Current role and top responsibility).
2.  **Past:** "Before that, I..." (Brief highlight reel of 1-2 past roles, emphasizing upward trajectory).
3.  **Future:** "What I'm looking for next is..." (Why this company and this role fit perfectly).

## Red Flags to Avoid
*   Complaining about past employers or managers.
*   Using "We" too much (hiding your individual contribution).
*   Rambling without structure (stick to STAR!).
*   Not having questions for them at the end.
