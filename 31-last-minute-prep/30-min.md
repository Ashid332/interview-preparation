# 30 Minutes Before: Warming Up

This is about loading context into your short-term memory, not learning anything new.

## 1. Review the Company and Role

*   **Interviewer(s):** Look at their names/LinkedIn one last time. Note their role (Frontend? Manager? Backend?).
*   **The Role:** Remind yourself of the top 3 requirements in the job description.
*   **Your "Why":** Mentally rehearse your answer to "Why do you want to work here?" and "Tell me about yourself."

## 2. Review Your Core Stories (Behavioral)

Don't reread everything. Just glance at your STAR matrix keywords:
*   *Conflict:* [Keyword for the story]
*   *Failure:* [Keyword for the story]
*   *Leadership:* [Keyword for the story]
*   *Complex Technical Challenge:* [Keyword for the story]

## 3. Warm-up Your Brain (Technical)

If you have a coding interview, do a simple "warm-up" exercise to get your fingers moving.
*   Write a quick FizzBuzz.
*   Write a standard BFS or DFS traversal from memory.
*   *Do not attempt a new or hard problem.* You just want to feel the syntax flowing.
