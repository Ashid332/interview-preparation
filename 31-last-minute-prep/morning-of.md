# The Morning Of

Start your day right. Set a calm, confident tone.

## 1. Physical Routine

*   [ ] **Hydrate:** Drink a large glass of water immediately upon waking.
*   [ ] **Eat:** Have a balanced breakfast (protein and complex carbs). Avoid sugar crashes.
*   [ ] **Caffeine:** Drink your normal amount of coffee/tea. Do not drink extra—it will make you jittery.
*   [ ] **Exercise:** Do some light exercise (a walk, stretching, light yoga) to get blood flowing.

## 2. Mental Routine

*   Read something completely unrelated to tech for 15 minutes to warm up your reading comprehension.
*   Do a quick vocal warm-up (sing a song in the shower, talk to a roommate/partner) so your voice is clear.

## 3. Final Review (Optional, Max 30 mins)

*   Read over your specific questions for the interviewers.
*   Glance at your one-page cheat sheets.
*   Review your "Tell me about yourself" elevator pitch.

## 4. The Golden Rule of the Morning

**No new information.** Do not look up Glassdoor questions, do not attempt LeetCode problems, do not read new system design articles. Trust the preparation you have already done.
