# The Night Before

Your primary goal tonight is rest and organization. Cramming will hurt you more than it helps.

## 1. The Logistics Sweep

*   [ ] **Outfit:** Choose your clothes (clean, comfortable, appropriate for the company culture). Lay them out.
*   [ ] **Tech Check:** Restart your computer. Install any necessary software (Zoom, Teams, etc.). Clear your desktop of clutter.
*   [ ] **Print/Prepare Materials:** Have a physical copy of your resume, a notepad, and a couple of good pens on your desk.
*   [ ] **Alarms:** Set two alarms.

## 2. Light Review (Max 1 Hour)

*   Review your **STAR stories matrix**. Ensure you have a story for the common themes (Failure, Conflict, Success, Leadership).
*   Review the **System Design Cheat Sheet** (CAP theorem, common database types, load balancing strategies).
*   Glance at your **language-specific cheat sheet** (e.g., JavaScript array methods).

## 3. Shut it Down

*   **Hard Stop:** Pick a time (e.g., 9:00 PM) to completely stop thinking about the interview.
*   **Distraction:** Watch a movie, read fiction, or play a game. Do not look at code.
*   **Sleep:** Aim for 8 solid hours. Sleep is when your brain consolidates memory and restores executive function.
