# 10 Minutes Before: The Final Countdown

Stop studying. Close the tabs. It's time to get into the zone.

## Physical & Setup Check

*   [ ] **Audio/Video:** Camera on, microphone tested, speakers working. Ensure lighting is good.
*   [ ] **Environment:** Phone on Do Not Disturb and out of reach. Door closed. Water glass full.
*   [ ] **Workspace:** Only necessary windows open (video call, blank notes, resume). Close all messaging apps.

## Mental Preparation

*   **Breathe:** Do 3 rounds of box breathing (4 seconds in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold).
*   **Power Pose:** Stand up, stretch your arms wide. Open your chest.
*   **Smile:** Force a smile. It changes your vocal tone to sound more energetic and confident.

## The 3 Things to Remember

1.  **They want to hire you.** The interviewer is hoping you are the right person so they can stop interviewing. You are on the same team.
2.  **Think out loud.** Silence is the enemy. Even if you are stuck, narrate your thought process.
3.  **It's a conversation, not an interrogation.** Engage, ask questions, and be a pleasant colleague.

## First Words

Have your introductory sentence ready:
> "Hi [Name], great to meet you! Thanks for taking the time to chat today. I'm really excited to learn more about the team."

**You are ready. Go get 'em.**
