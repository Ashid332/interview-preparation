# Engineering Interview Learning Paths

Welcome to the learning paths section of the Engineering Interview Handbook. This directory contains tailored, comprehensive 8-12 week preparation roadmaps for various engineering and data roles.

## Path Selector

| Role | Focus Area | Ideal For | Length |
|------|------------|-----------|--------|
| [Software Engineer](./software-engineer.md) | General SWE, Algorithms, System Design | Generalist developers, New Grads, general SWE roles | 12 Weeks |
| [Frontend Engineer](./frontend-engineer.md) | UI/UX, Browser APIs, React/Vue/Angular | Developers focusing on user-facing applications | 10 Weeks |
| [Backend Engineer](./backend-engineer.md) | APIs, Databases, Distributed Systems | Developers focusing on server-side logic and scale | 10 Weeks |
| [Full-Stack Engineer](./full-stack-engineer.md) | E2E Development, API integration, UI | Developers bridging frontend and backend | 12 Weeks |
| [AI Engineer](./ai-engineer.md) | LLMs, Prompt Engineering, AI Integration | Developers building applications on top of AI models | 8 Weeks |
| [Machine Learning Engineer](./machine-learning-engineer.md) | Model Training, MLOps, Data Pipelines | Engineers operationalizing machine learning models | 12 Weeks |
| [Data Scientist](./data-scientist.md) | Statistics, Predictive Modeling, A/B Testing | Professionals focused on extracting insights and modeling | 12 Weeks |
| [Data Analyst](./data-analyst.md) | SQL, BI Tools, Data Visualization | Professionals focused on reporting and business intelligence | 8 Weeks |
| [DevOps Engineer](./devops-engineer.md) | CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, Monitoring | Engineers focusing on deployment and reliability | 10 Weeks |
| [Cloud Engineer](./cloud-engineer.md) | AWS/GCP/Azure, Cloud Architecture | Engineers designing and managing cloud infrastructure | 10 Weeks |
| [Cybersecurity Engineer](./cybersecurity-engineer.md) | Threat Modeling, AppSec, Network Security | Engineers focused on securing systems and data | 10 Weeks |

## How to Use These Paths

1. **Assess Your Baseline**: Review the prerequisites for your chosen role.
2. **Commit to the Timeline**: Allocate 15-20 hours per week for study and practice.
3. **Follow the Milestones**: Track your progress using the provided checkpoints.
4. **Mock Interviews**: Integrate peer or professional mock interviews starting halfway through your path.
