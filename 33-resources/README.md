# Interview Preparation Resources

A curated list of high-quality resources for engineering interview preparation.

## Books
- **Cracking the Coding Interview (CTCI) by Gayle Laakmann McDowell:** The classic textbook for Data Structures and Algorithms.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA) by Martin Kleppmann:** The absolute gold standard for understanding distributed systems and databases. Crucial for Senior+ roles.
- **System Design Interview (Volumes 1 & 2) by Alex Xu:** Highly visual and practical guides to standard system design interview questions.
- **The Staff Engineer's Path by Tanya Reilly:** Essential reading for understanding the expectations and behavioral traits of Staff+ engineers.

## Courses & Platforms
- **LeetCode / HackerRank:** The standard platforms for practicing coding questions. (Focus on top interview questions and specific company tags).
- **NeetCode.io:** Excellent resource that organizes LeetCode questions into patterns (e.g., Sliding Window, Two Pointers) with video explanations.
- **Educative.io - Grokking the System Design Interview:** A very popular text-based course for system design fundamentals.
- **ByteByteGo:** Alex Xu's digital platform expanding on his System Design books.

## Tools
- **Excalidraw:** A fantastic virtual whiteboard tool. Practice drawing your system architectures here, as many remote interviews use similar tools.
- **Pramp / interviewing.io:** Platforms for doing mock interviews with peers or actual engineers from top tech companies.

## Blogs & Newsletters
- **High Scalability:** Case studies on how massive companies scale their architectures.
- **Engineering Blogs:** Read the engineering blogs of companies you are interviewing with (e.g., Netflix TechBlog, Uber Engineering, Meta Engineering) to understand their tech stack and scale.
- **Pragmatic Engineer Newsletter (Gergely Orosz):** Great insights into the tech industry, engineering levels, and interview processes.
